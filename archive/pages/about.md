# About Michele Welton | Your Purebred Puppy

![Ralph and Michele Welton with Buffy The Vampire Slayer](Graphics/author-family.jpg)

With my husband Ralph, and Buffy the Vampire Slayer

If you're looking for honest advice and information that will help you choose, raise, and train your companion dog, you've come to the right place.

We've been online for 18 years and are one of the oldest and most reputable web sites about dogs.

Hello! I'm Michele Welton and I have over 35 years experience as a dog breed advisor, obedience instructor, canine psychologist, veterinary technician, and author of 17 books about dogs, cats, and pet birds.

I've trained and shown dogs in competitive obedience, agility, herding, tracking, and schutzhund. I've worked with therapy dogs, police dogs, show dogs, and companion dogs.

I know dogs. So when I explain the pros and cons of different dog breeds, different dog foods, or different dog training methods, you're getting honest opinions and information based on a lifetime of experience and research.

I don't just follow the crowd. I tell you the truth as I see it. Honest advice about dogs is what our website is all about.

![Blue Chihuahua and cream Miniature Poodle](Graphics/bio5.jpg)

Mouse and Buffy. Mouse is a blue Chihuahua – yes, it really is called blue, even though it's gray. Buffy is a cream Miniature Poodle, clipped to look like a normal dog instead of the frou-frou look!

Here is our Miniature Poodle, Buffy The Vampire Slayer. Buffy is smart, obedient, and loves everyone (except vampires!). She was the mascot of my husband's third grade classroom for many years.

Beside her is our Chihuahua, Mouse. A rescue group diagnosed her with a liver tumor and skin disease, and scheduled her for euthanasia. So we stepped in and took her, and happily, we got those health problems under control.

Unhappily, Mouse was also born with a serious neurological disorder called syringomyelia, which reminds us each day just how precious and fragile life is.

## Trainer, Instructor, Consultant, Author

![Kelly and Princess](books/graphics/kelly-princess.jpg)

Kelly and Princess, the first dogs I ever trained, when I was a teenager. They were such happy dogs, as well as well-trained. I can show you how to have a happy, obedient dog, too.

I've loved and trained larger dogs, too. German Shepherds. Golden Retrievers. Doberman Pinschers. Beaucerons. Irish Wolfhounds. Norwegian Elkhounds. Great Danes. Beagles. Italian Greyhounds. And more.

In New England, I founded the **K-Mac Training Center**. In Southern California, I founded **The Canine Advisory**.

My advice on choosing, raising, and training dogs has been featured on the Orange County TV News Channel and in the Orange County Register newspaper.

I'm the author of 17 published books on dogs, cats, and pet birds.

And I've written every word of this 1250+ page web site, which has been online since 2000. Over 34 THOUSAND dog lovers visit us each day... that's over 12 MILLION dog lovers each year.

I'm so glad you've joined us!
