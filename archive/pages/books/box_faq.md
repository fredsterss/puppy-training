# Frequently asked questions about ebooks

Will you tell me how to download the ebook?

Yes, when you order, you'll receive an email with your download link. That link will take you to your personal download page, where you'll find step-by-step instructions for downloading.

  

How long does an ebook take to download?

The file size of an ebook is 3-5 MB. With a high-speed connection, downloading takes only a few seconds.

  

Do I need to be concerned about a computer virus or anything?

Absolutely not. My ebooks have been triple-checked to make sure they're completely safe, virus-free, and spyware free.

  

How do I read the ebook?

If you have a Windows or Mac computer or an Android mobile device, you can use Adobe Acrobat Reader. If you have an iPad or iPhone, you can also use Adobe Acrobat Reader, or you can use the iBooks app or Kindle app. All of these apps are free.

  

Do I have to come back to your website to read the ebook?

No, you'll save the ebook onto your own computer or mobile device. Then you'll always have it and can read it whenever you want.

  

Can I print the ebook if I want to?

Yes, but there are a LOT of pages! You might want to print just the pages you're most interested in.

  

If I have any trouble downloading, will you help me?

Yes, you can email me if you have any problems at all. And remember, there's a 30 day money back guarantee, so there's no risk.
