# Dog Breed Reviews: Tiny and Toy Dog Breeds

These breeds are the little guys, standing up to 12 inches at the shoulder, and weighing up to 10 pounds. Give or take.

My dog breed reviews include positives AND negatives – good and bad traits and characteristics – on temperament, personality, behavior, exercise requirements, shedding, etc.

Reviews are based on my personal and professional experiences – over 35 years working with dogs – plus my extensive research. I'm [Michele Welton](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/about.html) – breed selection consultant, obedience instructor, and published author of 15 dog books.
