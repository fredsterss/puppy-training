## Breed overview and temperament

American Water Spaniel temperament, personality, training, behavior, pros and cons, advice, and information, by [Michele Welton](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/about.html), Dog Trainer, Behavioral Consultant, Author of 15 [Dog Books](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/books/)

![American Water Spaniel dog breed](../dogbreeds/photos-AB/americanwaterspaniels3.jpg)

  

The AKC Standard says that the American Water Spaniel has "great energy and eagerness for the hunt."

This solidly built breed is happy, hardy, and adaptable but also energetic and in need of a good amount of daily exercise. Swimming and fetching are especially appreciated. Too much confinement or isolation can lead to hyperactivity and destructive behavior.

The temperament of the American Water Spaniel is about midway between that of an ingratiating [English Springer Spaniel](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/dogbreeds/englishspringerspaniels.html) and a self-reliant [Irish Water Spaniel](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/dogbreeds/irishwaterspaniels.html).

American Water Spaniels who have been extensively socialized are friendly. But many individuals make alert watchdogs and may not always welcome strangers into their home.

Most are fine with other family pets, though some can be dominant with strange dogs.

The American Water Spaniel is a bit of a challenge to train. He has a stubborn streak and is not as eager to please as some other spaniels. At the same time he is highly sensitive and may respond defensively if he thinks he is being treated unfairly (whether he actually is or not!) Consistent leadership is a must.

  

If you want a dog who...

- Is medium-sized and solidly built, with a wavy brownish coat
- Is more discriminating with strangers than most spaniels, and makes a better watchdog
- Loves vigorous athletic activities
- Is hardy and healthy

An American Water Spaniel may be right for you.

  

If you don't want to deal with...

- Providing lots of exercise
- "Separation anxiety" (destructiveness and barking) when left alone too much
- Suspiciousness toward strangers if not socialized enough
- Stubbornness (mind of his own)
- Vocalness - barking, whining, and yodeling
- Waiting lists (hard to find)

An American Water Spaniel may not be right for you.

Keep in mind that the inheritance of **temperament** is less predictable than the inheritance of **physical** traits such as size or shedding. Temperament and behavior are also shaped by raising and training.

- You can avoid some negative traits by choosing an ADULT dog from an [animal shelter](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/animal-shelters.html) or [rescue group](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-rescue.html). With an adult dog, you can easily see what you're getting, and plenty of adult American Water Spaniels have already proven themselves not to have negative characteristics.
- If you want a puppy, you can avoid some negative traits by choosing the [right breeder](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breeders.html) and the [right puppy](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/how-to-choose-a-puppy.html). Unfortunately, you usually can't tell whether a puppy has inherited temperament or health problems until he grows up.
- Finally, you can avoid some negative traits by [training your American Water Spaniel](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/articles/dog-respect-training.html) to respect you and by following the 11-step care program in my book, [11 Things You Must Do Right To Keep Your Dog Healthy and Happy](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/books/11T.html).

More traits and characteristics of the American Water Spaniel

If I was considering an American Water Spaniel, I would be most concerned about...

1. **Providing enough exercise and mental stimulation.** American Water Spaniels were developed to be hunting dogs. They must have regular opportunities to vent their energy and do interesting things. Otherwise they will become bored, which they usually express by barking and destructive chewing. You should be an active owner who enjoys long daily walks, hiking, swimming, or visits to the dog park.
2. **Potential separation anxiety.** More than most other breeds, American Water Spaniels need a great deal of companionship. They do not like being left alone for more than a few hours. They tend to express their unhappiness through destructive chewing and barking. If you work all day, this is not the breed for you.
3. **Providing enough socialization.** Standoffish by nature, American Water Spaniels need extensive exposure to people and to unusual sights and sounds. Otherwise their natural caution can become shyness or suspiciousness, which are difficult to live with.
4. **Mind of their own.** American Water Spaniels can be stubborn and manipulative. You must show them that you mean what you say. To teach your spaniel to listen to you, "Respect Training" is mandatory. My [American Water Spaniel Training Page](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/) discusses the program you need.
5. **Potential barking.** American Water Spaniels are often too quick to sound the alarm at every new sight and sound. You have to be equally quick to stop them before this behavior becomes a bad habit.
6. **Finding one.** American Water Spaniels are rare. You will almost certainly need to go on a waiting list, whether you want a puppy from a breeder or an adult dog from a rescue group.

## Are YOU the right owner for an American Water Spaniel?

Can you provide what this breed needs?

- Someone home most of the day
- Fenced yard (not an electronic/underground fence)
- Ample exercise after maturity – enough ongoing exercise that your American Water Spaniel stays slim and is tired enough to sleep contentedly and not get into mischief
- "Mental exercise" – interesting activities that keep the mind stimulated, such as a challenging dog sport (agility, rally obedience, flyball, musical freestyle, dock diving, tracking, field trials); challenging dog toys; a homemade obstacle course; tricks and games such as Musical Toys and Hide 'n Seek; instructions in my training book, [Teach Your Dog 100 English Words](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/books/TYD.html)
- Brushing – moderate
- Trimming – every few months
- An indoor lifestyle, except for exercise and bathroom breaks
- A meat-heavy diet, either [homemade](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/health/articles/feeding-homemade-dog-food.html) or [commercial](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/health/articles/feeding-healthy-natural-dog-food.html) – meat is expensive, so people with less money should opt for a smaller dog
- An owner with enough money to treat the [health problems](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/health/americanwaterspaniels.html) American Water Spaniels are prone to (pet health insurance can really help here!)
- An owner who is okay with moderate shedding
- Commitment to provide thorough socialization – introducing your American Water Spaniel to lots of people and other animals
- Commitment to establish the proper Leader-Follower relationship with your American Water Spaniel, teaching him to [listen to you and do what you say](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/articles/dog-respect-training.html)

## Where can you buy or adopt an American Water Spaniel?

American Water Spaniels are very uncommon in the United States. Out of 189 breeds in the American Kennel Club, where 1 is most popular and 189 is least popular, American Water Spaniels rank 156th. Their dark curlyish coat may not be attractive to a lot of people, although I think they're handsome.

[Adopting From Dog Rescue Organizations](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-rescue.html)  
Not many American Water Spaniels are turned over to Rescue groups, because not many people own them in the first place. Also the breed is not that difficult to live with, so fewer owners tend to give up on them and dump them into Rescue.

[Adopting From Public Animal Shelters and Humane Societies](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/animal-shelters.html)  
American Water Spaniels are virtually never found here for the same reasons listed above. Plus, American Water Spaniel Rescue groups keep their eyes peeled on shelters and humane societies across the country. On the off chance that an American Water Spaniel turns up at a shelter, the rescue group typically moves in quickly to take the dog.

[Buying From a Dog Breeder](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breeders.html)  
You can buy an American Water Spaniel from a *show breeder*, who breeds American Water Spaniels to match a detailed standard of appearance for the dog show ring. Or you can buy an American Water Spaniel from a *performance breeder*, who breeds "field dogs" strictly for hunting. Some breeders are a combination of *show/performance*, though how they prioritize those two goals can vary greatly.

Here's one difference between a responsible breeder and an irresponsible breeder – BOTH PARENTS of an American Water Spaniel puppy should have:

- a certificate from the Canine Eye Registry Foundation (CERF) – dated within the past year – certifying the dog to be free of **eye diseases**
- a certificate from the Orthopedic Foundation of America (OFA) or PennHip certifying the dog to have normal **hips**
- a certificate from the Orthopedic Foundation of America (OFA) certifying the dog to have a normal **thyroid**
- a certificate from the Orthopedic Foundation of America (OFA) or a report from a veterinary cardiologist – dated within the past year – certifying that the dog has had an *Advanced Cardiac Exam* and has a normal **heart**

Also, at least ONE PARENT of an American Water Spaniel puppy should have:

- a DNA test proving they are Normal/Clear of a severe hereditary eye disease called **prcd-PRA**.

If a seller can't show you those certificates, the puppies are higher risk for health problems. You might choose to accept that risk. But then you need to be willing (and able) to pay a couple thousand bucks for future surgeries and lifelong meds if your American Water Spaniel ends up with heart disease and hip dysplasia.

Dog training is much less about the teaching of commands and much more about how you live with your dog. Virtually all of your dog's behaviors (good and bad) are created by the way you're living with him. What you do with him... what you don't do with him... what you allow him to do... these are all messages to your dog. Send the right messages and you will have no problems with your dog. Send the wrong messages and you will have problems.

## Complete list of American Water Spaniel health problems

Skin and ears are problematic in this breed. Ear infections are common, as well as chronic allergies that cause itchy skin and can progress to bacterial skin infections. A hair loss disease (follicular dysplasia) also occurs in American Water Spaniels.

Bad joints (hips and knees) cause pain and lameness that can require expensive surgery. The Orthopedic Foundation of America evaluated the hip X-rays of 580 American Water Spaniels and found 8% have hip dysplasia.

The most serious eye disease in American Water Spaniels is progressive retinal atrophy, which causes blindness. Cataracts and retinal dysplasia can also cause blindness.

Epilepsy, heart disease, and thyroid disease are occurring with increasing frequency in American Water Spaniels.

Degenerative myelopathy, which progresses to rear-leg paralysis, can also occur in the American Water Spaniel.

### Preventing health problems

Some health problems are inherited. For example, if your dog inherits from his parents the genes for an eye disease called PRA, he will go blind and there's nothing anyone can do about it.

## Frequently asked questions

By [Michele Welton](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/about.html), Dog Trainer, Behavioral Consultant, Author of 15 [Dog Books](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/books/)

![American Water Spaniel](../dogbreeds/photos-AB/americanwaterspaniels-big1.jpg)

The American Water Spaniel is the state dog of Wisconsin. Hunters appreciated this smallish retriever who would plunge readily into the icy Great Lakes and not tip the boat over when he climbed back in!

  

What kind of temperament and personality does the American Water Spaniel have?

The most descriptive words that come to my mind regarding American Water Spaniel temperament and behavior are:

lively, hardy, loyal, on the stubborn side, proud, sensitive

I give you my honest opinions about American Water Spaniel temperament and personality traits and characteristics – positives AND negatives – in my dog breed review, [American Water Spaniel Temperament and Personality Traits](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/reviews/americanwaterspaniels.html) (What's Good About 'Em, What's Bad About 'Em).

What were American Water Spaniels used for?

American Water Spaniels were developed in the Great Lakes region of the US. These versatile hunters can work like spaniels, searching through fields and flushing hiding game birds such as grouse and quail. They also work like retrievers, plunging fearlessly into icy bays to fetch birds shot by their owner.

What traits would help American Water Spaniels accomplish their work? That's right... a good nose, liveliness, ruggedness, athleticism, biddability, and willingness to work closely with his owner.

How big are American Water Spaniels? Height and weight?

About 15-18 inches at the shoulder, and 30-45 pounds, which puts them in the [MIDSIZE](../buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-size.html#midsize) category.

What colors do American Water Spaniels come in?

Shades of brown, from liver to chocolate.

How much [exercise](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-exercise.html) do American Water Spaniels need?

A lot. The athletic American Water Spaniel is bred to work and loves being outdoors in natural settings. This is not a city dog, nor a dog for someone who just wants a casual pet. If you don't provide enough exercise, an American Water Spaniel will vent his energy through barking and destructive chewing. DO active things with your American Water Spaniel, every day.

Do American Water Spaniels need a fenced yard?

Yes. [Why?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/do-dogs-need-fenced-yard.html) and [best and worst fences](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-fencing.html).

Are American Water Spaniels [easy to train?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/articles/dog-respect-training.html)

Moderate, I would say. Unlike most spaniels, this breed can be somewhat resistant, even stubborn. But they generally do want to please, so as long as you can establish the right leader-follower relationship, they should be fine.

Are American Water Spaniels easy to housebreak?

They're about average, no real problems.

Are American Water Spaniels friendly with people?

This loyal breed loves his family, sometimes even bonds with just one person. With strangers, American Water Spaniels are usually standoffish, may even be suspicious.

Are American Water Spaniels [good with children?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-children.html)

American Water Spaniels have an innate sense of pride and fairness and may react defensively if a pushy child steps over the line. I much prefer to see American Water Spaniels (and all spaniels, really) in homes with kids who are at least about age 10 and can be relied upon to treat a dog gently and respectfully.

Are American Water Spaniels good [with other dogs?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-aggression-dogs.html)

American Water Spaniels are usually okay with familiar dogs. They might act assertively toward other dogs of the same sex.

Are American Water Spaniels good [with cats?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-aggression-cats.html)

They're usually fine with cats in their own family.

How much do American Water Spaniels [shed?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-shedding.html)

American Water Spaniels are average shedders, which means moderate shedding in the spring and fall, and occasionally a tiny bit of hair the rest of the year.

Are American Water Spaniels [hypoallergenic?](../buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-shedding.html#allergic) Good for people with allergies?

If you're allergic to dog dander, no. If you're allergic to canine saliva, no. American Water Spaniels are not hypoallergenic dogs.

How much [grooming](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-grooming.html) do American Water Spaniels need?

The American Water Spaniel's coat is a mass of crisp, coarse ringlets, which may be tightly curled or just loosely waved in an undulating pattern. His head and face are smooth, though. Brush once a week and occasionally trim straggly hairs.

What's a good training schedule for training American Water Spaniel puppies?

Here's the puppy training schedule I use for American Water Spaniels: [Puppy Training Schedule](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/articles/puppy-training-schedule.html).

What behavior problems do American Water Spaniels tend to have?

Most commonly I see excessive barking and whining. Sometimes possessiveness of food or toys. Destructive chewing if you don't provide enough exercise. Here's [how to handle behavior problems](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/articles/dog-behavior-problems.html).

What [health problems](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/health/americanwaterspaniels.html) might American Water Spaniels have?

Chronic itchy skin and ears. Loose hip and knee joints can cause lameness. Eye diseases can cause blindness.

What breeds are similar to the American Water Spaniel?

![Boykin Spaniel](../dogbreeds/photos-AB/boykin-aws-faq.jpg)

Boykin Spaniel, the state dog of South Carolina

The Boykin Spaniel is very similar – not surprising given that the Boykin was developed by crossing American Water Spaniels with other hunting breeds. The Boykin's home community is Boykin, South Carolina. Just as the AWS is the state dog of Wisconsin, the Boykin is the state dog of South Carolina.

I have not worked with many Boykins or American Water Spaniels – they're so uncommon. But of those I have, the Boykins were more outgoing and playful, while the AWSs were more aloof and more rugged.

[Irish Water Spaniel](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/reviews/irishwaterspaniels.html). This medium-sized to large water dog needs even more exercise and mental stimulation than the American Water Spaniel. Irish Water Spaniels do best with experienced owners.
