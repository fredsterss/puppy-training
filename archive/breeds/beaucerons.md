## Breed overview and temperament

Beauceron temperament, personality, training, behavior, pros and cons, advice, and information, by [Michele Welton](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/about.html), Dog Trainer, Behavioral Consultant, Author of 15 [Dog Books](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/books/)

![Beauceron dog breed](../dogbreeds/photos-AB/beauceronsf4.jpg)

  

This old French breed may look like a cross between a German Shepherd and a Doberman Pinscher or Rottweiler, but it is most definitely purebred! I've owned Beaucerons myself.

This is a confident, steady-tempered, usually serious dog.

Athletic and agile, he needs hard exercise (running, hiking, fetching). A walk around the block is most assuredly not enough. Too much confinement and too little exercise can lead to destructive behaviors and rambunctiousness, especially in youngsters.

Mental exercise (advanced obedience, agility, herding, Schutzhund) is just as important to this highly intelligent breed.

Matching his stern appearance, the Beauceron is usually keen-eyed and watchful, aloof and discriminating with strangers. Socialization must be early and frequent so that his watchfulness doesn't shade into suspiciousness or aggression. Shyness is unfortunately present in some lines.

Most Beaucerons are territorial with other animals, but usually good with the pets in their own family, if raised with them.

Beaucerons were bred to be herding dogs, so they might try to poke or push people and other animals – this is an attempt to move them along or gather them together.

Indeed, Beaucerons like to control everyone and everything. They require a confident, consistent owner who knows how to lead.

  

If you want a dog who...

- Is large, athletic, and ruggedly handsome
- Has a short easy-care coat
- Looks stern and imposing, and makes a vigilant watchdog

A Beauceron may be right for you.

  

If you don't want to deal with...

- Vigorous exercise requirements
- Rowdiness and exuberant jumping, especially when young
- Destructiveness when bored or not exercised enough
- Aggression or fearfulness toward strangers in some lines, or when not socialized enough
- Aggression toward other animals
- Strong-willed mind of his own, requiring a confident owner who can take charge
- Chasing and nipping at things that move: children, joggers, other animals, bikes, cars
- Waiting lists (hard to find) and a high price tag

A Beauceron may not be right for you.

Keep in mind that the inheritance of **temperament** is less predictable than the inheritance of **physical** traits such as size or shedding. Temperament and behavior are also shaped by raising and training.

- You can avoid some negative traits by choosing an ADULT dog from an [animal shelter](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/animal-shelters.html) or [rescue group](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-rescue.html). With an adult dog, you can easily see what you're getting, and plenty of adult Beaucerons have already proven themselves not to have negative characteristics.
- If you want a puppy, you can avoid some negative traits by choosing the [right breeder](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breeders.html) and the [right puppy](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/how-to-choose-a-puppy.html). Unfortunately, you usually can't tell whether a puppy has inherited temperament or health problems until he grows up.
- Finally, you can avoid some negative traits by [training your Beauceron](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/articles/dog-respect-training.html) to respect you and by following the 11-step care program in my book, [11 Things You Must Do Right To Keep Your Dog Healthy and Happy](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/books/11T.html).

More traits and characteristics of the Beauceron

If I was considering a Beauceron, I would be most concerned about...

1. **Providing enough exercise and mental stimulation.** Beaucerons MUST have regular opportunities to vent their energy and do interesting things. Otherwise they will become rambunctious and bored – which they usually express by barking and destructive chewing. Bored Beaucerons can make a shambles of your house and yard.

   I don't recommend this breed if you want a casual pet for your family. Beaucerons were never intended to be simply household pets. Get them involved in herding, or tracking, or agility (obstacle course), or advanced obedience, or schutzhund (protection), or carting, or a similar canine activity. They must have physical outlets for their energy and mental outlets for their intelligent minds.
2. **Providing enough socialization.** Most Beaucerons have protective instincts toward strangers. They need extensive exposure to friendly people so they learn to recognize the normal behaviors of "good guys." Then they can recognize the difference when someone acts abnormally. Without careful socialization, they may be suspicious of everyone, which could lead to biting. Some Beaucerons go in the opposite direction – without enough socialization, they become fearful of strangers, which can lead to **defensive** biting.
3. **Potential animal aggression.** Beaucerons can be fine with the pets in their own family. But many individuals are dominant or aggressive toward strange dogs of the same sex. Some have strong instincts to chase and seize cats and other fleeing creatures.
4. **The strong temperament.** The best Beaucerons are versatile working dogs, capable of learning a great deal, but they have an independent mind of their own and are not pushovers to raise and train. Some Beaucerons are very dominant (they want to be the boss) and will make you prove that you can make them do things.

   To teach your Beauceron to listen to you, "Respect Training" is mandatory. My [Beauceron Training Page](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/) discusses the program you need.
5. **Potential health problems.** Bone and joint disorders are much too common in Beaucerons. You need to avoid irresponsible breeders who haven't x-rayed their dogs' hips and elbows before breeding them. See [Beauceron Health](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/health/beaucerons.html) for more advice.
6. **Finding one and paying the price.** This breed is not common and breeders are charging a lot for them.

## Are YOU the right owner for a Beauceron?

Can you provide what this breed needs?

- Someone home most of the day
- Fenced yard (6-8 feet high, not an electronic/underground fence)
- No dogs of the same sex in the household
- Restricted exercise when young – until maturity (at least 18 months old), exercise restricted to multiple short (20 minute) walks, fetch games, and playing with other dogs – no forced running (beside a jogger or bicyclist), no long-distance treks, minimal jumping
- Ample exercise after maturity – enough ongoing exercise that your Beauceron stays slim and is tired enough to sleep contentedly and not get into mischief
- "Mental exercise" – interesting activities that keep the mind stimulated, such as a challenging dog sport (agility, rally obedience, schutzhund, herding, tracking, carting); challenging dog toys; a homemade obstacle course; tricks and games such as Musical Toys and Hide 'n Seek; instructions in my training book, [Teach Your Dog 100 English Words](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/books/TYD.html)
- An indoor lifestyle, except for exercise and bathroom breaks
- A meat-heavy diet, either [homemade](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/health/articles/feeding-homemade-dog-food.html) or [commercial](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/health/articles/feeding-healthy-natural-dog-food.html) – meat is expensive, so people with less money should opt for a smaller dog
- An owner with enough money to treat the [health problems](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/health/beaucerons.html) Beaucerons are prone to (pet health insurance can really help here!)
- An owner who is okay with moderate shedding
- Commitment to provide thorough socialization – introducing your Beauceron to lots of people and other animals, diligently correcting any signs of misbehavior or aggression
- Commitment to establish the right Leader-Follower relationship with your Beauceron, teaching him to [listen to you and do what you say](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/articles/dog-respect-training.html)

## Where can you buy or adopt a Beauceron?

Beaucerons are not a common breed in the United States. Out of 189 breeds in the American Kennel Club, where 1 is most popular and 189 is least popular, Beaucerons rank 140th.

[Adopting From Dog Rescue Organizations](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-rescue.html)  
You might find a Beauceron available from a Dog Rescue group, although it's not that common. Beaucerons may be turned over to Rescue because they need too much exercise or because the they're too much for the owner to handle. You would need to provide these dogs with the exercise, training, and socialization that they are lacking.

[Adopting From Public Animal Shelters and Humane Societies](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/animal-shelters.html)  
Beaucerons are very rarely found here. Beauceron Rescue groups keep their eyes peeled on shelters and humane societies across the country. On the off chance that a Beauceron turns up at a shelter, the rescue group typically moves in quickly to take the dog.

[Buying From a Dog Breeder](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breeders.html)  
You can buy a Beauceron from a *show breeder*, who breeds Beaucerons to match a detailed standard of appearance for the dog show ring.  
  
Or you can buy a Beauceron from a *performance breeder*, who emphasizes an energetic temperament and strong "prey (chasing) drives" for participating in protection dog sports (schutzhund and ringsport). Some breeders are a combination of *show/performance*, though how they prioritize those two goals can vary greatly.  
  
You might also be able to buy a Beauceron from people who "just breed pets" or "just had one litter." But should you? Be sure to read the article to learn more about these people.

Here's one difference between a responsible breeder and an irresponsible breeder – BOTH PARENTS of a Beauceron puppy should have:

- a certificate from the Canine Eye Registry Foundation (CERF) – dated within the past year – certifying the dog to be free of **eye diseases**
- a certificate from the Orthopedic Foundation of America (OFA) or PennHip certifying the dog to have normal **hips**
- a certificate from the Orthopedic Foundation of America (OFA) or a report from a veterinary cardiologist – dated within the past year – certifying that the dog has had an *Advanced Cardiac Exam* (including an ECHO) and has a normal **heart**

If a seller can't show you those certificates, the puppies are higher risk for health problems. You might choose to accept that risk. But then you need to be willing (and able) to pay a couple thousand bucks for future surgeries and lifelong meds if your Beauceron ends up with hip dysplasia and heart disease.

Beauceron Training and Puppy Training, by [Michele Welton](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/about.html), Dog Trainer, Breed Selection Consultant, Author of 15 [Dog Books](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/books/)

Dog training is much less about the teaching of commands and much more about how you live with your dog. Virtually all of your dog's behaviors (good and bad) are created by the way you're living with him. What you do with him... what you don't do with him... what you allow him to do... these are all messages to your dog. Send the right messages and you will have no problems with your dog. Send the wrong messages and you will have problems.

## Complete list of Beauceron health problems

Hip dysplasia is the major health problem. The Orthopedic Foundation of America evaluated the hip X-rays of 500 Beaucerons and found 12% dysplastic. Elbow dysplasia is at 4%.

Other orthopedic problems in Beaucerons include osteochondritis and panosteitis.

Eye diseases include progressive retinal atrophy (PRA), cataracts, and eyelid abnormalities (entropion and ectropion).

Hypothyroidism, blood-clotting disease (von Willebrand's), and cancer are increasing concerns in Beaucerons.

Allergies cause itchy skin that often leads to bacterial skin infections (pyoderma).

With their deep chest, Beaucerons are at higher-than-normal risk for the emergency gastrointestinal syndrome called bloat.

### Preventing health problems

Some health problems are inherited. For example, if your dog inherits from his parents the genes for an eye disease called PRA, he will go blind and there's nothing anyone can do about it.

[![Dog feeding and health book by Michele Welton](../books/covers/11T-110.jpg)](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/books/11T.html) But most health problems can be prevented by the ways you raise your dog.

## Frequently asked questions

By [Michele Welton](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/about.html), Dog Trainer, Behavioral Consultant, Author of 15 [Dog Books](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/books/)

![Beauceron](../dogbreeds/photos-AB/beauceronsf1.jpg)

The Beauceron on the right has the natural ears she was born with. The Beauceron on the left has had his ears "cropped" (surgically cut) to stand erect, to make him look more fierce. This painful procedure is banned in most of the world... not the US, though. Not yet.

  

What kind of temperament and personality does the Beauceron have?

The most descriptive words that come to my mind regarding Beauceron temperament and behavior are:

athletic, serious, watchful, loyal, strong chasing/poking instincts

I give you my honest opinions about Beauceron temperament and personality traits and characteristics – positives AND negatives – in my dog breed review, [Beauceron Temperament and Personality Traits](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/reviews/beaucerons.html) (What's Good About 'Em, What's Bad About 'Em).

How do you pronounce Beauceron?

BO-ser-on. *Beauce* (Bose) is a large agricultural and livestock region in France.

What were Beaucerons used for?

Beaucerons drove and guarded sheep (and sometimes cattle) in France. They also guarded the family farm, hunted wild boar, and served as messenger dogs and explosives detection dogs in World War I.

What traits would help Beaucerons accomplish their work? That's right... high energy, endurance, athleticism, toughness, independent thinking yet high trainability, and loyalty to their owner.

How big are Beaucerons? Height and weight?

About 24-28 inches at the shoulder, and 70-100 pounds, which puts them in the [LARGE](../buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-size.html#largesize) category.

What colors do Beaucerons come in?

Only two colors. By far the most common is black, with tan or rust markings (like a Doberman or Rottweiler).

Less common is *harlequin,* which starts with the same base color of black with rust/tan markings, then covers most of it with greyish/bluish/whitish patches. In other breeds, this color pattern is called *merle.*

Do Beaucerons come in different "types"?

Yes. Beaucerons bred primarily for exhibition in the dog show ring are often larger and heavier, with a "softer" temperament.

Beaucerons bred for working purposes are more energetic, athletic, and agile. Those used in protection dog sports also have an intense, aggressive temperament, while those used in Search & Rescue are non-aggressive.

A few breeders produce Beaucerons for hunting big game such as wild boar. As you might expect, these dogs are very tough and animal-aggressive.

How much [exercise](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-exercise.html) do Beaucerons need?

A lot. The athletic Beauceron won't sit quietly in your home or yard if you don't provide enough exercise and challenging mental stimulation.

Like all large working breeds, a Beauceron needs to be involved in activities such as herding, carting, advanced obedience, agility, protection dog sports, jogging, hiking, tracking and scent work.... DO lots of stuff with a Beauceron, or please get a different breed.

Do Beaucerons need a fenced yard?

Yes. [Why a fenced yard?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/do-dogs-need-fenced-yard.html) and [best and worst fences](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-fencing.html).

Are Beaucerons [easy to train?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/articles/dog-respect-training.html)

Yes and no. If you are a confident, assertive person who can "read" and respond to a strong-minded working dog and establish a [healthy leader-follower relationship](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/articles/dog-respect-training.html), the Beauceron can learn almost anything and is a delight to work with. Otherwise... well, this breed is not for beginners.

Are Beaucerons easy to housebreak?

Yes. I've never had any housebreaking issues with Beaucerons.

Are Beaucerons friendly with people?

Beaucerons are devoted to their own family, and standoffish with strangers.

Many Beaucerons have [protective instincts](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-aggression-people.html), but without actual protection training,  they are not effective protectors. But they can still be excellent deterrents... as long as you stay in charge and immediately stop any unwarranted aggression.

At the other extreme, some Beaucerons are timid, nervous, or genetically shy. So be careful of your lines in this breed.

Are Beaucerons [good with children?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-children.html)

Beaucerons raised with children are usually gentle and tolerant. Otherwise, it really depends on the individual dog and the individual kids. Be aware that Beaucerons may try to herd running children by poking at them.

Are Beaucerons good [with other dogs?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-aggression-dogs.html)

If you're a strong leader, Beaucerons are usually fine with other dogs in their own family. Still, it's safest to keep a Beauceron with a companion of the opposite sex. Toward the same sex, Beaucerons can be dominant, and if the other dog disagrees, a serious fight may ensue.

Are Beaucerons good [with cats?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-aggression-cats.html)

My Beaucerons were fine with our own cat – indoors – once she bopped them on their noses once or twice. But outdoors, they would go after any cat (or any other small animal) they spied in our yard, with deadly intent.

How much do Beaucerons [shed?](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-shedding.html)

Beaucerons are average shedders. Their coat sheds moderately in the spring and fall, and occasionally a tiny bit of hair the rest of the year.

Are Beaucerons [hypoallergenic?](../buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-shedding.html#allergic) Good for people with allergies?

If you're allergic to dog dander, no. If you're allergic to canine saliva, no. Beaucerons are not hypoallergenic dogs.

How much [grooming](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/buying/articles/dog-breed-traits-grooming.html) do Beaucerons need?

Just occasional brushing... more often when shedding.

What's a good training schedule for training Beauceron puppies?

Here's the puppy training schedule I use for Beaucerons: [Puppy Training Schedule](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/articles/puppy-training-schedule.html).

What behavior problems do Beaucerons tend to have?

The critical puppy and adolescent periods are when you must teach calmness, establish the right leader/follower relationship, and respond properly to [behavior problems](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/training/articles/dog-behavior-problems.html). Do those things right, plus provide enough exercise and mental stimulation, and your Beauceron will be much less likely to have any behavior issues at all.

Occasional issues with my own Beaucerons centered around them poking/pushing children and other pets in an attempt to move them along or gather them together. I also kept them under complete control when in the presence of other large dogs of the same sex.

What [health problems](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/health/beaucerons.html) might Beaucerons have?

Loose joints can cause lameness and crippling arthritic pain. Eye diseases can lead to blindness. And gastric torsion (bloat) is always a worry in large dogs with deep chests, like Beaucerons.

What breeds are similar to the Beauceron?

To the untrained eye, the Beauceron looks rather like a blend of Doberman Pinscher, Rottweiler, and German Shepherd.

- Compared to a [German Shepherd](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/reviews/germanshepherds.html), the Beauceron is healthier and more sensitive.
- Compared to a [Rottweiler](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/reviews/rottweilers.html), the Beauceron is healthier, more sensitive, and more energetic.
- Compared to a [Doberman](https://www.yourpurebredpuppy.com/reviews/dobermanpinschers.html), the Beauceron is healthier and calmer.

If you're looking for a show dog, buy from a show breeder. If you're looking for an active working Beauceron, buy only from a working breeder. If you're looking for a companion dog... I stick with breeders who show their dogs in conformation AND work them in performance activities. [Read my article.](../buying/beaucerons.html#where)
